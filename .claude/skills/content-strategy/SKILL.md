# Content Strategy

## Gather first (ask only what is missing)
- Business or profile, offer, and main goal (sales, leads, authority, followers)
- Target audience: who they are, their problems, where they hang out
- Channels and posting capacity per week
- Current assets: past posts that worked, brand voice, competitors

## Build
1. **Positioning**: one sentence on who you help and how you are different.
2. **Pillars**: 3 to 5 themes, each tied to an audience problem or a business goal.
3. **Formats**: map each pillar to formats suited to the channel (Reels, carousel, long post, video, email).
4. **Funnel balance**: roughly 60% attract (reach), 30% nurture (trust), 10% convert (offer).
5. **Calendar**: a 4-week plan with date, pillar, format, hook, call to action.
6. **Hooks and angles**: 10 hook options per pillar, specific rather than generic.
7. **Measurement**: 3 to 5 metrics, review cadence, and what to change when a post wins or fails.

## Rules
- Specific beats generic: use real examples, numbers and the audience's own words.
- Fewer channels done well beats many done badly.
- Deliver in the user's language and keep the calendar copy-pasteable (table).
